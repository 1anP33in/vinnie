Dragon settings are saved with Vinnie's other settings and survive restarts. Change them in the viewer
(**Viewer settings** button in the panel header, applied immediately) or under **Settings → Ice dragon**.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the ice dragon | On | Off: no 3D viewer is loaded |
| Layout | Side panel | Side panel beside the chat, or a compact strip above it |
| Graphics quality | Medium | See [Performance settings](/dragon/performance-settings/) |
| Snow | On | Falling snow |
| Ice sparkles | On | Occasional glints of ice in the air |
| Fog | On | Soft distance fog |
| Lighting effects | On | Cyan glow, rim lights, floor reflections (High quality), and shadows |
| Idle animations | On | Breathing, blinking, and glancing around while idle. Task postures still change when paused |
| Follow the cursor | On | While idle, the dragon glances toward the mouse pointer |
| Animation speed | 100% | 25% to 200% |

The panel size and the camera position are remembered too. **Reset camera** returns to the default view.

## Camera

- Drag in the viewer to rotate around the dragon; scroll to zoom.
- The zoom buttons are in **Viewer settings**.

## Reduced effects

With **Settings → Appearance → Reduce visual effects** on, or when Windows asks apps to reduce motion, the dragon
shows no particles, glow, or cursor following. Its postures still follow the agent.
