---
title: "Animation Settings"
description: "Control the dragon's motion and visual effects."
sidebar:
  order: 4
---
**Settings → Appearance → Reduce visual effects** turns off glow, frost, and animations throughout the app,
including the pulsing of the dragon mark in the status bar.

:::caution[Planned]
Settings specific to the animated dragon viewer (on/off, size, quality) are planned with the viewer itself and
don't exist yet.
:::
